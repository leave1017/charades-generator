Before you write

A few things come up often enough that the answer is already on the site, and you will get it faster by clicking than by waiting for a reply:

  • → "How do I actually play?" The rules, the hand signals and the timing are all on How to Play Charades.
  • → "Can I print the words instead?" Yes — the printable card builder lays them out nine to a page with cut lines already drawn.
  • → "A word is wrong / too hard / not appropriate." Please do write — tell us the word and which page you saw it on. Words get removed on this basis regularly.
  • → "Who runs this?" That is on the About page.
